## Preservation and execution rules

- Keep PostgreSQL, all customer/account/row/transaction IDs, identity mappings, balances, financial and audit history, deletion/tombstone state, pending/offline mutations, receipt bytes, original HMAC/encryption keys, and key/recovery metadata. Reconcile with before/after manifests on protected copies; never silently merge, regenerate, omit, truncate, or delete records or keys. Resolve collisions by reviewed business meaning and retain non-equivalent rows.
- Keep email/phone OTP and supported Google sign-in. Do not add a customer password, require a recovery-key file, or silently upload data. Automatic account recovery remains opt-in and disabled until its acceptance gates pass. Do not broaden the current recovery quota by assumption.
- Introduce readers before writers; retain legacy readers and old keys through the full data and backup recovery lifetime. Backfills must be bounded, resumable, compare-and-swap protected, and report progress without customer data in Git. A rollback after new-format writes must use compatible readers or forward repair, never restore a stale plaintext snapshot over newer writes.
- The [I07 notification/I09 shared-feedback source slice](security-durable-delivery-implementation-2026-10-26.md) is now integrated and verified locally behind default-off flags. Coverage, provider/custody/operational acceptance and other route classes remain; neither whole package nor related launch gate is complete.
- Separate local implementation and synthetic/clone evidence from device, provider, account-owner, legal, custody, live-host and production acceptance. Do not configure or send to real providers, change live data/ACLs, activate features, purchase services, or deploy under this plan.
