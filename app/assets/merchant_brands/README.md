# Merchant marks

These PNGs are transparent masks. The app supplies the foreground and surface
colors at render time: each brand's own colour is in
`lib/app/design/merchant_brand_colors.dart` (a brand with no entry is black or
white in its artwork and gets a neutral tile), and `tile_tones.dart` turns it
into a soft tint with a mark that stays readable on light, dark and slate.
Add the colour when you add the artwork. Add the corresponding enum value and exact name aliases
in `lib/app/design/merchant_brand.dart` when adding artwork. Use a standalone
symbol if available, trim empty margins, and check it on a 34 dp tile.

- Most masks are from [Simple Icons 16.37.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.37.0/icons).
  Adobe, Amazon, Canva, Flipkart, OpenAI, and Prime Video are from
  [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons).
  See the [project license](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/LICENSE.md)
  and [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/DISCLAIMER.md).
- The Blinkit and Zepto wordmarks were extracted from the SVGs in
  [Quickcom Darkstores](https://github.com/Sidshot/Quickcom_Darkstores/tree/0b06fd8bcf840155779f13f84e8ef3f21aebbb5b/assets).
- JioHotstar uses the star from the [JioStar media toolkit](https://www.jiostar.com/mediatoolkit/all-logos/).
  Myntra uses the mark [sourced from Myntra's artwork](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Myntra_Logo.png).

Brand marks identify their respective merchants and services.
