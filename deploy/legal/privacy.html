PandaPay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what data PandaPay collects, why, and what you can do about it. We are committed to collecting only what the app actually needs to function.

1. Account data

If you create an account, we retain the account identifiers needed for email or phone sign-in and supported Google sign-in. We do not sell this data. Account data is stored on our servers with access controls, and traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.

2. Card and transaction data

We do not ask you to save your full card number, PIN or CVV. Card or account last-four digits may be stored to match the cards and transactions you choose to add. Your card products and transactions are scoped to your account and used to estimate rewards and track spending, balances and caps.

3. How you can add transactions

You can add transactions manually or import statement and backup files you choose. On supported Android devices, enabling SMS import and granting permission lets the app read existing and incoming messages for financial parsing; unrelated personal messages are not intended for upload. Depending on the feature you use, financial message content or derived records can be sent to our backend for processing. If enabled and configured, server-assisted AI processing can send redacted financial message content to the configured AI provider. Optional shared-learning contributions use a separate opt-in. You can also forward transaction emails or connect IMAP for automatic import. Receipt images and unsynced records may exist only on your phone. If encrypted account recovery is available and you enable it, the app can upload encrypted recovery copies, including receipts when you select them, and update them automatically while connectivity and app activity permit. Recovery uses your existing account verification and adds no separate password. Work created after the last successful copy remains at risk if the phone is lost before another copy completes.

4. Location

If you grant location access, your precise location may be sent to the PandaPay API for nearby searches or enabled background alerts. The API can forward search coordinates to Google Places or OpenStreetMap-based providers, depending on the configured route. This feature is optional. Stored merchant location coordinates are rounded to roughly 11 metres; that rounding does not describe the precision sent for a nearby search.

5. Crowdsourced contributions

Contributions (e.g. confirming a merchant accepts a card, or correcting a category) are stored without your name, email, account ID, or any other identifying field — enforced by an automated check that runs before every release and blocks deployment if it finds an identifying column. Merchant coordinates are rounded to ~11m, amounts are excluded entirely, and timestamps are stored to the day only, never the minute. Internally, contributions are linked across a single calendar month using a one-way hash that is rotated and discarded at the end of each month, so contributions from different months cannot be linked to each other or back to you.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token tied to your account to deliver reward alerts, due-date reminders, and cap warnings. It is deleted when you delete your account.

7. Product analytics

We collect basic in-app usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, whether an action succeeded) from a fixed, limited list of event types — never free-text input, transaction amounts, or card details. This stays on our own servers; it is not shared with third-party analytics companies.

8. Card issuer "Apply" links

Tapping "Apply" for a card sends you to the issuer's own site via a link containing a random reference code, not your personal information. We record that the click happened (to measure which recommendations are useful); the issuer does not receive your name, email, or any other account data from us.

9. Data access choices

We do not read your contacts or call log. Access to selected receipt images, statement files and backups is used for the import or recovery actions you choose. You can control optional Android SMS access through device permissions and the import settings described above.

10. Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. Requesting deletion from Settings starts a 30-day grace period (cancelable any time in that window, in case of accidental or unauthorized requests) after which your profile, cards, and transactions are permanently deleted from our live database. Encrypted backups are retained separately for disaster recovery and age out on their own schedule, so a small delay beyond the 30 days can occur before every backup copy is gone.

11. Your rights

You can export your data (transactions and cards, as JSON or CSV) or request deletion at any time from Settings. If you are in India, these rights are provided consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. Children

PandaPay is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

14. Contact
